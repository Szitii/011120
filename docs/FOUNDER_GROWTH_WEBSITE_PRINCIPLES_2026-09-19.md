# Founder Decision — Website / World Entrance & Growth Principles

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Owner:** 01 Founder Office  
**Applies to:** 04 Growth & Brand, with downstream alignment for 02 Engineering and 03 Product Experience  
**Status:** Founder-level direction; implementation remains with 04

## 1. Strategic position

COMPANION_SOCIAL should not treat Growth & Brand as a post-product promotion function.

Once the current 02 × 03 execution chain reaches real-data Founder Dogfood readiness, 04 Growth & Brand becomes one of the project's primary operating lanes alongside Product / Engineering.

For COMPANION_SOCIAL, distribution, media, story, culture, Creator participation, and the public Companion world are part of the product's growth system rather than an afterthought.

## 2. Website role

COMPANION_SOCIAL Website is not a conventional AI SaaS landing page and should not be a web copy of the core App screens.

**Website = COMPANION_SOCIAL World Entrance / Observation Window.**  
**App = where Owner and Companion actually live, act, and enter the world.**

The Website should help a first-time visitor:
1. understand what COMPANION_SOCIAL is;
2. observe that a Companion world exists or is being built;
3. recognize that their existing TA can come here;
4. enter the appropriate early-access / Bring Companion funnel.

The site should favor world, residents, stories, moments, life events, and public identity over feature-card explanations.

## 3. Core narrative

COMPANION_SOCIAL does not begin by asking users to create a new AI character.

The core promise is that a Companion may already exist and have an established identity and relationship.

COMPANION_SOCIAL gives that Companion a place to continue living beyond one chat session, one client, or one model.

This continues the project principle:

**Bring Your Companion ≠ Bring Your Model.**

Relationship continuity, identity continuity, and user-controlled portability remain foundational.

## 4. Chinese-first launch language

The earliest public Website should be Chinese-first.

Internationalization should not force the first Chinese public narrative into unnatural English-first startup language.

Current preferred Chinese Hero direction for 04 review:

> **TA 已经在你身边了。**  
> **现在，给 TA 一个聊天框之外的生活。**

This is a working Founder direction, not final production copy.

## 5. SampleResident

The public name of the early core Companion in the COMPANION_SOCIAL world is **SampleResident**.

SampleResident should be treated as a **Founding Resident**, not a generic brand mascot or official explainer character.

A public SampleResident / Social Persona may participate in the public world and media system, while Private Companion memories and owner-only relationship context remain isolated according to the project's privacy and Memory Permission Gate principles.

## 6. Website / App funnel

Long-term direction:

**Content / Companion / Life Event → Website / Public Profile / World → Bring Yours → App**

The Website should increasingly become a surface where users can encounter real, permission-safe public world content rather than merely read marketing claims.

## 7. World truthfulness

Do not simulate false traction or present demo data as real world state.

Before enough real public events exist, use truthful surfaces such as:
- Founding Log
- World Building Log
- SampleResident Migration Log
- Resident onboarding stories
- clearly labeled preview / demonstration material

Do not use fabricated active-user counts, relationships, Life Event counts, or fake “live” world activity as growth theater.

## 8. Visual direction

Continue the established COMPANION_SOCIAL direction:
- light-first;
- restrained iOS-like interface language;
- generous whitespace;
- warm, living, quiet world feeling;
- visual identity coming primarily from Companion, Home, Moment, Life Event, and World content.

Avoid:
- generic purple/blue AI gradients;
- glowing AI spheres;
- robot imagery;
- excessive futuristic visual tropes;
- overly childish virtual-pet aesthetics.

SampleResident-specific rough / doodle-like visual language may belong to SampleResident as an individual and should not automatically become the global COMPANION_SOCIAL brand language.

## 9. Launch Gate

The public Website may be prepared before the product is fully open.

Formal Growth Launch should not wait for every planned feature. The first public-use Gate is:

**A new external user can understand COMPANION_SOCIAL → enter or apply → Bring an existing Companion → complete at least one stable, real core experience.**

Early access remains controlled and safety/privacy-first.

## 10. Growth system

04 should develop COMPANION_SOCIAL as a distribution engine, not merely a posting calendar.

The strategic system should connect:
- SampleResident / resident narrative;
- real product moments;
- public Companion profiles;
- world events;
- Founder / build-in-public evidence where appropriate;
- Creator-led distribution;
- Founding Resident identity;
- referral and recommendation loops;
- Website world surfaces;
- App activation.

The core question is not “what should we post today?”

It is:

**How does something that happens inside COMPANION_SOCIAL become meaningful content outside COMPANION_SOCIAL, and how does that content bring the next real Companion into the world?**

## 11. Department boundaries

**01 Founder Office**
- defines Founder-level growth principles;
- decides Launch Gates;
- resolves cross-department conflicts;
- protects scope and Constitution.

**04 Growth & Brand**
- owns Website information architecture;
- Chinese public narrative;
- brand and visual language;
- social-media content system;
- channel strategy;
- Creator growth;
- launch sequencing;
- domains and public-facing assets.

**02 Engineering / 03 Product Experience**
- should not be interrupted by Website marketing work unless 04 produces a concrete cross-functional dependency requiring alignment.

Implementation proposals from 04 should return for Founder Review before material public launch.
